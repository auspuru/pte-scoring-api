# Speaking mock background assessment

`SPEAKING_PROGRESSIVE_SCORING=true` enables progressive assessment. New attempts pin `progressive-v1`, Azure delivery and the Academy DI content backend. Turning the flag off stops new enrollment; workers continue pinned attempts. Writing scoring and the task-weighted Speaking formula are unchanged.

Each successful recording upload enqueues an idempotent answer job. A periodic cursor scan closes the save/enqueue crash gap, submits expired mocks, and resumes pending work without a browser. PostgreSQL claims use leases, fencing tokens and `SKIP LOCKED`. Successful stages are saved in small job records; original audio stays in the existing attempt. Workers fetch one recording at a time. Progress polling excludes all base64 audio. Verified stages are merged into a submitted attempt once, preserving concurrent instructor overrides and recordings.

Content and Azure delivery must be valid for every question before publication. Explicit skips, unvisited questions at expiry and confirmed zero-content gates remain in the denominator as zero. Missing, provisional and stale marks remain pending. API detail, history and recording review withhold the report until the Azure report is complete. Students see a preparing banner and automatic status polling; no scoring Retry button is required. Five to ten minutes is an expectation, not a provider SLA.

DI uses `VISUAL_ACADEMY_SCORING_URL` and a scoped `PTE_DI_SERVICE_KEY`, shared only with Academy. It sends verified image facts, the saved automatic transcript and normalized WAV. It does not forward account sessions or provider keys. The Academy service uses its canonical combined content/coaching reviewer for content and coaching. Pronunciation and fluency use Azure in PTE; Academy is never asked to produce GPT audio marks. The overall combines verified Academy content with local Azure delivery. A prepared receipt is persisted before submission; lost responses recover by the same job ID. Remote hashes and contract versions are checked before accepting marks.

Workers retain successful companion traits and Azure chunk transcripts/results. Known temporary failures and validation failures have a bounded three-attempt budget with backoff. Service transport retries are idempotent. A stale in-flight paid call or explicitly uncertain outcome is held for staff review, because another provider call could incur a duplicate charge. Authentication/configuration failures are also held. These cases never become zero marks and never require a student to resubmit audio.

Admin diagnostics: `GET /api/admin/speaking-mocks/:username/:id/scoring` using the existing admin authentication. Held jobs also emit structured `scoring-job` logs containing IDs and failure codes, never audio or transcripts. A staff repair uses `POST` to the same path plus `/retry`, with `{jobId, stage, operation}`. `operation` is a unique audit ID; repeating it does not rescore. Only held stages can be retried. After inspecting provider usage, an uncertain call additionally requires `acknowledgeUncertain:true`. For an Academy failure the repair is forwarded to its authenticated internal route first. The student page continues polling throughout.

Deploy the backward-compatible Academy service and verify authenticated capabilities before enabling PTE. Existing valid historical reports are retained without bulk paid regrading. Incomplete historical mocks resume when opened; pending mocks also resume in the background scan. Account deletion cascades to PostgreSQL answer jobs.

Validation covers upload-time scoring, complete-only publication, cross-account protection, automatic provider retry, restart recovery, idempotent DI handoff, approved delivery policy, durable PostgreSQL claims and UI transition from waiting to the full report.

Delivery assessment in progressive mocks keeps content coverage separate from audio sufficiency. A sufficiently long partial Repeat Sentence response can receive pronunciation and fluency marks; its missing words still reduce its native content mark. All audio evidence validators continue to apply. Individual practice keeps its existing eligibility policy.

Azure's per-answer pipeline shares the whole-mock audio boundary handling. A final segment shorter than four seconds is joined to its predecessor when the pair stays below 30 seconds, or the pair is divided evenly. Every PCM sample and internal pause is retained. Existing successful earlier chunks are reused.

For an explicitly scoped operational repair, `IPT_SPEAKING_BACKGROUND_RECOVERY` accepts `usernames`, a unique `operation`, and optional `action:'repair'` with `repairs:[{username,attemptId,jobId,stages}]`. Diagnostics are the default. Repairs allow only known held Azure failures, validate ownership and saved recording hashes before changing any job, and retain an operation receipt so restarts cannot rescore the same repair. Uncertain outcomes remain held for explicit staff investigation. Diagnostics omit recordings, transcripts, credentials and session tokens. A `speaking-mock-published` event confirms the Azure overall score and full question coverage after publication succeeds.

## Administrator email alerts

`SPEAKING_MOCK_ALERTS_ENABLED=true` and `SPEAKING_MOCK_ALERT_EMAIL` enable a one-minute watchdog. It checks submitted attempts, including older attempts with durable answer jobs. It sends an email when a required assessment is held for staff review, or when a complete report has remained unpublished for ten minutes. Active tests and complete reports do not trigger notifications. Before sending, it rechecks the current attempt and can publish already-complete saved marks without another provider assessment.

For HTTPS delivery on Railway, set `SPEAKING_MOCK_ALERT_TRANSPORT=resend`, a sending-only `RESEND_API_KEY`, and `SPEAKING_MOCK_ALERT_FROM` to a sender email on a Resend-verified domain. Add the key directly in Railway Variables, never in chat or source control. Apply the variables and redeploy. Resend's `onboarding@resend.dev` testing sender is limited to the Resend account owner's recipient address; use it only when that owner matches the configured alert recipient. The API endpoint is fixed to `https://api.resend.com/emails`; redirects are rejected. Other portal emails retain their existing configuration.

Without an explicit transport, `auto` selects Resend when either HTTPS setting is present, otherwise the existing `GMAIL_USER` and `GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD` SMTP account. `smtp` can also be selected explicitly. Railway permits SMTP only on Pro and higher plans; HTTPS email APIs work on all plans (see [Railway outbound networking](https://docs.railway.com/networking/outbound-networking)). Missing HTTPS settings pause queued notifications without consuming their send-attempt budget, so deployment after configuration resumes them automatically if the report is still unresolved.

Emails contain the username, paper, attempt ID, submission time in Brisbane, missing questions/providers, safe failure codes, suggested checks and authenticated admin links. They exclude recordings, transcripts, scores and credentials. The only recipient is the configured administrator; no student email is sent.

`speaking_mock_alert_jobs` is a persistent outbox with one record per attempt, fenced leases and an accepted-email receipt. Restarting or scanning the same attempt cannot send another alert. The exact email body is frozen before sending. HTTPS retries, including a restart at the send boundary, use the same Resend idempotency key and body. Replay stops after 23 hours to remain inside Resend's 24-hour guarantee; an expired or reconfigured uncertain request is held for review. Temporary failures retry with bounded backoff. An uncertain SMTP DATA outcome remains held instead of blindly sending a duplicate. A report resolved while its email is queued cancels the notification. Account deletion cascades to its notification records. The monitor does not reset provider retry budgets or alter scoring.

`GET /api/admin/speaking-mock-alerts` uses the existing admin authentication and exposes the selected provider, configuration and transport health without secrets. SMTP startup verifies its connection without sending a test message. HTTPS startup validates local settings only and reports `configuration-only`, with `verified:false`, until an email is accepted. This avoids requesting full-access provider credentials for a sending-only integration. `speaking-alerts` logs distinguish `transport_ready`, `transport_verification_failed`, `email_accepted` and delivery failures. Acceptance means the mail provider accepted the request, not proof of inbox delivery. Startup does not send a sample email unless explicitly requested. Delivery depends on provider credentials, sender verification and network access; a complete application/database outage requires separate infrastructure monitoring.

To send an authorized sample, `POST /api/admin/speaking-mock-alerts/test` with existing admin authentication and `{operation:"unique-test-reference"}`. The recipient and sender are fixed by the alert configuration; the endpoint accepts no recipient override or arbitrary message. The subject starts with `[TEST]`, and the body states that no scoring incident was created. The sample uses the same durable delivery/retry/idempotency function as real alerts, with a separate `admin_alert_test_jobs` outbox that contains no student records or account foreign key. Repeating the same operation, including after restart, cannot send another sample. An explicitly configured `SPEAKING_MOCK_ALERT_TEST` operation also queues the sample once after production startup for an owner-controlled deployment test. Clear it after confirmation. Release tests remove this variable and all mail credentials, and never send a sample.

## Azure-only delivery policy

Pronunciation and fluency in practice and mocks use Microsoft Azure exclusively. New profiles require only `azure`. Read Aloud/Repeat Sentence content alignment, the Academy DI content reviewer, other task content reviewers and transcription retain their existing roles. No production controller calls the retired GPT audio scorers or whole-audio GPT recovery. The student API excludes old GPT comparisons and totals; archived judgments remain in storage. Publication and alerts examine content plus Azure for every question, including skips and confirmed content gates. Old GPT-only holds are retired with fenced updates; saved native Azure and content stages are reused. A genuine held Azure/content request retains its staff-review and uncertain-call safeguards.

Visual Academy uses the existing shared service key to call the authenticated `/internal/v1/academy-azure` service in this portal. `POST /jobs` accepts verified PCM WAV, its hash and the automatic transcript. Azure jobs and audio are stored separately, deduplicated by a stable ID and fingerprint, and processed with chunk checkpoints and bounded retries. `GET /jobs/:id` exposes only status, receipt and native results. An authenticated `POST /jobs/:id/retry` accepts a unique `operation`, and requires `acknowledgeUncertain:true` for an uncertain Azure request after staff investigation. Audio expires a day after terminal completion, or after three days at most; job receipts expire after 90 days. Azure credentials stay in this service. The Academy holds its receipt, polls without a new provider request, and combines completed Azure marks with saved content.

Deploy this portal first, then Academy. The Academy startup probe verifies authenticated Azure capabilities without consuming speech credits. Historical Academy GPT scores remain stored but are withheld by the current view; a new recording supplies Azure evidence because original Academy recordings were not retained. No bulk paid reassessment is performed.
