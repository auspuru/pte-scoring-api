# Writing Sectional Mock essay map

Each prediction paper contains the complete Writing sectional mix used by this app: two Summarise Written Text questions, one essay, one Summarise Spoken Text question and three Write From Dictation questions. Scores are reported as a practice estimate out of 90.

| Sectional mock | Essay topic | Essay question |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Television as a Relaxation Tool and Companion | Television serves many useful functions. It helps people to relax. Besides, it can also be seen as a companion for the lonely. To what extent do you agree with this? Explain why with your own experience. |
| 02 | Studying Old Plays and Theatre Works | What are the problems and the benefits for high school students of studying plays and other works for theatre that were written centuries ago? Do you agree with it? Use your own experience to discuss it. |
| 03 | Combining Study and Employment | Effective study requires time, comfort and peace. It is impossible to study with employment because one may distract the other. To what extent do you think the statements are realistic? Give your opinion with examples. |
| 04 | Experiential Learning in Education | Some people point that experiential learning (i.e. learning by doing it) can work well in formal education. However, others think a traditional form of teaching is the best. Do you think experiential learning can work well in high schools or colleges? |
| 05 | Digital Materials and Libraries | With the increase of new digital media available online, the role of the library has become obsolete. Therefore universities should only procure digital materials rather than constantly update textbooks. Discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this position and give your own point of view. |
| 06 | Public Transport and Road Building | As cities expand, governments should look forward to creating better networks of public transport available for everyone rather than building more roads for vehicle owning population. To what extent do you agree or disagree? |
| 07 | Tourism in Less Developed Countries | For a less developed country, the disadvantages of tourism are as great as the advantages. Please discuss this statement, and give and explain your opinion. |
| 08 | Formal Written Examinations | Many education systems assess students' learning using formal written examinations. Those kinds of exams are a valid method. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give examples with your own experience. |
| 09 | Compulsory Foreign Language Learning | Some people think learning a foreign language at school should be compulsory. To what extent do you agree with it? Use your experience or examples to support your viewpoint. |
| 10 | The Most Pressing Global Problem | In today's world, different government and international organisations are confronting many global problems. What is the most pressing problem among them and give solutions? |
| 11 | Medical Technology and Life Expectancy | The medical technology can increase the average life expectancy. Do you think it is a curse or a blessing? |
| 12 | Parental Legal Responsibility | Should parents be held legally responsible for the actions of their children? Support your opinion from your study, observations or experiences. |
| 13 | Building Design and Daily Life | Do you think the design of buildings affects, positively or negatively, where people live and work? |
| 14 | Mass Media Influence on Young People | The mass media, such as TV, radio and newspapers, have an influence on people, particularly on younger generations. It plays a pivotal role in shaping the opinions of people, especially teenagers and young people. To what extent do you agree with this? Please give examples. |
| 15 | Modern Inventions and Their Impact | In our technological world, the number of new inventions has been evolving on a daily basis. Please describe a new invention and determine whether it brings beneficial or detrimental impact to society. |
| 16 | Workers in Decision-Making | In some companies, employers involve workers in decision-making process about products and services. What are the advantages and disadvantages of such a policy? |
| 17 | Responsibility for Tackling Climate Change | Climate change is a concerning global issue. Who should take the responsibilities, governments, big companies or individuals? |
| 18 | Laws and Human Behaviour | Some people think human behaviours can be limited by laws, and others think laws have little effect. What is your opinion? |
| 19 | Shopping Malls Replacing Small Shops | Large shopping malls are replacing small shops. What is your opinion on this? Do you think this is a good or bad change? |
| 20 | Youth Unemployment and Shorter Working Week | Unemployment among young people is a serious problem. One solution has been suggested is to shorten the working week. What do you think are the advantages and disadvantages? Do you think this policy should apply to just young workers or the whole workforce? |
| 21 | Fewer Working Hours in the Future | In the future, people will work fewer hours at their jobs than they do now. Do you agree with the statement? Please support your opinion with your own experience. |
| 22 | Maximum Wage for High-Paying Jobs | Some people say there should be a maximum wage for high-paying jobs. Do you support that? Can you give your point of view or your own experience? |
| 23 | Famous People and the Right to Privacy | People who are famous entertainers or sportspeople should give up the right to privacy, because this is the price of fame. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this point of view? Give your opinion with your experiences. |
| 24 | Studying Climate Change | Imagine you have been assigned on the study of climate change. Which area of climate change will you focus on and why? Use examples. |
| 25 | Work-Life Balance | Nowadays, it is increasingly more difficult to maintain the right balance between work and the other aspects of one's life, such as leisure pursuits with family members. How important do you think this balance is? What are the reasons that make some people think that this is hard to achieve? |
| 26 | Experience is the Best Teacher | Some people argue that experience is the best teacher. Life experiences can teach more effectively than books or formal school education. How far do you agree with this idea? Support your opinion with reasons and/or your personal experience. |
| 27 | AI and Foreign Language Learning | While artificial intelligence becomes so advanced, people can use computers to translate foreign languages that makes learning a foreign language unnecessary. To what extent do you agree with it? |
| 28 | Travel and Quality of Education | Some believe the value of travel is overrated. One brilliant scholar never leaves the home bases. People argue whether travel is a necessary component of quality education or not. To what extent do you agree with it? |
| 29 | City vs Countryside Living | Some people prefer to live in cities, while some people prefer to live in the countryside. Which is better for you? Give your reasons or experience. |
| 30 | Growing Up in the 21st Century | It is harder for children to grow up in the 21st century than it was in the past. How far do you agree with this statement? Give your opinions. |
| 31 | Historic Buildings vs Modern Housing | Many countries spend large amounts of money on the restoration of historic buildings instead of on modern housing. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this analysis? What are advantages and disadvantages of this? Support your writing with your experience or examples. |
| 32 | Communication Methods in Modern Society | The means of communicating in society today has changed markedly over the last ten years. In your opinion, what are the positive and negative impacts of this change? |
| 33 | The Value of Humanities | Some say that in today's world the value of humanities has been eclipsed by the necessity of preparing for specific wealth-producing careers, such as medicine. Discuss whether you think there is a role in today's changing world for study of the humanities. |
