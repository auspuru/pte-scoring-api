## Priority reference proposals

These are proposed editorial replacements. The audit records the affected IDs and versions. Teacher-authored records and frozen paper reference payloads retain their existing history.

| Exact passage version | Evidence and proposed replacement |

| Farm Cottage, short | “I was trying to persuade her.” Change the key from “persuaded” to “tried to persuade”. The expanded sample should continue to distinguish his familiarity from her greater need for trust. |
| Human Remains | “effectively removed from the curation process.” Replace the reversed key with “Reburial removes the remains from curation and prevents future investigation with improved methods.” Museum-wide reburial was a concern. | Economic Forecasting, short | “they do better for some stable statistics, such as government spending” precedes the separate GNP/inflation clause. Use “Corporate economists use statistical data, but differing assumptions limit forecast precision, and forecasts are especially poor for volatile measures such as interest rates, with better results for some stable measures such as government spending.” The stable concession can be omitted with volatile scope and the precision explanation intact. |

## Scoring and feedback

Full Content requires accurate main meaning, necessary relationships and meaningful integration or reformulation. A supported connector can express a previously implicit link. Changing synonyms or joining unchanged clauses alone leaves an otherwise strong answer at C3. Omitted optional examples incur no deduction. Content, Form, Grammar and Vocabulary retain 4/1/2/2 maxima and the existing zero gates. A clear sentence can earn full language and Form marks with weak Content. Harmless slips receive optional advice.

The candidate requires a verified student span and an exact source quotation for Content deductions, together with a simple add, remove or replace instruction. An example revision is shown when the edit produces a response within the existing Form checks. Independent review and adjudication still apply. Semantic correctness of generated revisions needs a provider check as well as these evidence and Form checks.

The standalone API's separate Writing contribution estimate of 90 at 8/9 is a heuristic stored with practice progress. The student result panels derive their estimates independently of that field. The new SWT item value drives the standalone item display and Writing Lab per-question display. The previous standalone panel and Writing Lab conversion produced 81 at 8/9, while the standalone headline already capped it at 79. Writing Lab's section and task-type rollups retain native points. Full Mock uses the same native result in Reading and Writing with their existing profile weights.

New standalone and Writing Lab results save the policy and source/reference identity. Full Mock records native SWT policy identity with the attempt's paper and scoring snapshot. Saved marks and the previous item conversion stay associated with their original result. The exact Full Mock compatibility projection retains the earlier policy and provider for older compatible snapshots, including pending assessments.
