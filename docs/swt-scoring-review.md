## Scoring and feedback

Full Content requires accurate main meaning, necessary relationships and meaningful integration or reformulation. A supported connector can express a previously implicit link. Changing synonyms or joining unchanged clauses alone leaves an otherwise strong answer at C3. Omitted optional examples incur no deduction. Content, Form, Grammar and Vocabulary retain 4/1/2/2 maxima and the existing zero gates. A clear sentence can earn full language and Form marks with weak Content. Harmless slips receive optional advice.

The candidate asks for the shortest useful part of the passage to keep and a targeted edit that paraphrases or connects it in simple English. Seventy-five words is a maximum. Near the limit, the grader can replace a longer phrase or pair one edit with a separate cut of optional detail. The portal applies the suggested change to the whole answer before showing it, checks that it fits the existing Form requirements, and shows the new word count and any words saved. The full example opens separately. An over-limit edit goes back for correction within the existing review process.

For example, in a 75-word Forecasting response, replace “however, it falls far short of being a precise science,” with “however, differing assumptions limit its precision,”. This states why the forecasts are imprecise and brings the answer down to 71 words. The source supports it with “their assumptions about how an economy works can vary widely”.

Relevant fragments with no useful supported proposition earn at most **1/4 Content**. Where some useful propositions communicate partial understanding, the existing lower bands apply. Form and language traits are assessed under their respective rules. Independent review and adjudication still apply. Semantic accuracy and naturalness of model-written edits need a provider check in addition to the evidence and Form checks.

The standalone API's separate Writing contribution estimate of 90 at 8/9 is a heuristic stored with practice progress. The student result panels derive their estimates independently of that field. The new SWT item value drives the standalone item display and Writing Lab per-question display. The previous standalone panel and Writing Lab conversion produced 81 at 8/9, while the standalone headline already capped it at 79. Writing Lab's section and task-type rollups retain native points. Full Mock uses the same native result in Reading and Writing with their existing profile weights.

New standalone and Writing Lab results save the policy and source/reference identity. Full Mock records native SWT policy identity with the attempt's paper and scoring snapshot. Saved marks and the previous item conversion stay associated with their original result. The exact Full Mock compatibility projection retains the earlier policy and provider for older compatible snapshots, including pending assessments.
