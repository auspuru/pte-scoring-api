Orientation helps new employees understand how an organisation works before they are expected to perform independently. A useful program explains responsibilities, safety, workplace policies, communication channels and where to obtain technical or personal support. Meeting colleagues and managers early can reduce uncertainty and help newcomers understand how their role connects with the wider team. Too much information in one session is easily forgotten, so important guidance should remain accessible afterwards. Effective orientation is a process of integration rather than a single presentation on the employee’s first day.
